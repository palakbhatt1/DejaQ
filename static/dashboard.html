System Design

Pipeline Architecture & Safeguards

DejaQ employs a 3-stage cheapest-first evaluation with independent coverage planning.

STAGE 01 · O(1) LOOKUP

Exact Normalization

NFKC Unicode normalization, lowercase, punctuation stripping, and hash table lookup.

STAGE 02 · FAST VECTORS

Cosine + Numeric Guard

bge-small-en-v1.5 FastEmbed embeddings. Numerical discrepancies force candidate to the judge or review.

STAGE 03 · LLM JUDGE

Borderline Resolution

Gemini Flash evaluates similarity band [0.75, 0.96) with strict task-differentiation rules.
